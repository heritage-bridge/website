07 — Our Story

We began with one church
and a borrowed camera.

Heritage Bridge started as a student initiative documenting a single damaged church in Mosul. The first model was imperfect. The response was not: archivists, engineers and families who had prayed there wrote to ask for more.

Today we work across Iraq and Armenia with universities and cultural institutions, building an open record of places that cannot document themselves.
